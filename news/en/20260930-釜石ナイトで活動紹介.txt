Our Disaster Rugby Volunteer Work Presented at "Kamaishi NIGHT"
On Wednesday, September 30, our president Hirotaka Sonobe was invited as a guest speaker at "Kamaishi NIGHT" (#167), a monthly gathering hosted by Scrum Kamaishi at the No Side Club in Takadanobaba, Tokyo.
Scrum Kamaishi was formed after the Great East Japan Earthquake to support rugby in Kamaishi, and our president took part in its early gatherings. This time he spoke about how the project began in 2011 by presenting furaiki flags to rugby teams in Tohoku, the day 120 furaiki flew at the Rugby World Cup 2019 in Kamaishi, and the disaster rugby volunteer work we continue in Noto and Kumamoto.
Masatoshi Mukoyama, head of The Kids Sports Exchange, which runs the international exchange program KIDS TRY 2026, also spoke as a guest that evening.
images/news/20260930-kamaishi-night.jpg | From left: Sonobe, Jiro Ishiyama (head of Scrum Kamaishi), and Masatoshi Mukoyama (head of The Kids Sports Exchange)
It was a valuable opportunity to speak directly to a room full of rugby fans about conditions in the affected areas and our goal of "rugby players showing up wherever disaster strikes." We thank Scrum Kamaishi and the No Side Club for having us.

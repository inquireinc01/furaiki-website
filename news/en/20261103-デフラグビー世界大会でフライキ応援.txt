We Waved Furaiki Flags to Cheer On the Players at the 3rd Deaf Rugby World Sevens
On Tuesday, November 3 (a national holiday), we took part as event volunteers on the final day of the 3rd Deaf Rugby World Sevens TOKYO 2026 at Prince Chichibu Memorial Rugby Ground.
Deaf rugby is rugby played by deaf and hard-of-hearing athletes. To deliver cheering that players can see rather than hear, we waved our big-catch furaiki flags in the stands at every try and at the final whistle.

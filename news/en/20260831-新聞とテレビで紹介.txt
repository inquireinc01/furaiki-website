Our Work Featured in Print and on Television
On August 31, 2026, the Hokkoku Shimbun and Toyama Shimbun (Kanazawa edition, page 23) carried a feature on our members' relief work following the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake, under the headline "To Kumamoto with the Spirit of a Rugby Player." It follows a member who has made 70 individual trips to the disaster areas of Noto, and who spent August removing collapsed block walls in Yatsushiro, Kumamoto.
Around the same time, NHK Kumamoto's viewer mail segment introduced photographs of our work, sent in by a resident of Kumamoto City under the title "Volunteers from Kanazawa." They came from someone we had worked alongside on site.

We are grateful to everyone who noticed our work and passed it on.

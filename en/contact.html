Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum donation amount?

You can donate from 500 yen. We offer several options, including monthly recurring donations and one-time donations in an amount of your choice.

How can I participate as a volunteer?

Please contact us using the inquiry form first. We share activity updates through our official LINE account. No rugby experience is required, and we welcome volunteers from across Japan at any time.

How are donations utilized?

Donations are used for on-site activity costs, such as transportation and accommodation, as well as Furaiki flag production and organizational operating expenses. We regularly publish reports detailing how funds are used.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Furaiki Project is not currently an approved or specially approved corporation engaging in specified non-profit activities. Accordingly, the tax incentives available for donations to such corporations, including donation deductions, do not currently apply. We will consider applying for approval once we meet the requirements. Please consult your local tax office or a tax adviser regarding your individual tax treatment.

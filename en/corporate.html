CSR Partnerships, Sponsorships, and Joint Projects

CSR Partnerships

Furaiki Project draws on the network and ability to act of people with rugby backgrounds to support the emotional recovery of disaster-affected communities. Companies and organizations can partner with us as part of their corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs in many ways, including sending employee volunteers, funding our activities, and providing supplies. We invite you to join us as a partner in addressing social challenges.

Sponsorship Options

Financial Sponsorship

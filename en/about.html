On the Front Lines of Disaster Relief

Our Activities

From the Great East Japan Earthquake in 2011 to the present, Furaiki Project has walked alongside people in disaster-affected communities. Our work goes beyond providing supplies and removing debris; emotional recovery has always been our highest priority.

Members of the rugby community gather from across Japan, united by the No Side spirit: once the match is over, there are no enemies or allies, and everyone moves forward together. By bringing this shared value to our work, we have built deep relationships with disaster-affected communities.
